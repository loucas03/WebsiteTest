My Lemon Cake

A simple, fresh and delicious homemade cake.

About this recipe

This lemon cake is soft, light and full of fresh lemon flavor. It's perfect with a cup of tea or coffee.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 150 g sugar
  • 100 g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C.
  2. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly.
  3. Mix the eggs and sugar until smooth.
  4. Add the butter and the juice and zest of the lemon.
  5. Stir in the flour, baking powder and salt.
  6. Pour the batter into a cake pan.
  7. Bake for about 30–35 minutes.
  8. Let the cake cool before serving.

My tip

For an extra lemony flavor, add a little lemon zest on top before serving.
