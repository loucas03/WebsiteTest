🍋 My Lemon Cake

A simple, fresh and delicious homemade cake.

About this recipe

This lemon cake is soft, light and full of fresh lemon flavor. It's perfect with a cup of tea or coffee.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 150 g sugar
  • 100 g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C.
  2. Melt the butter and let it cool slightly.
  3. Mix the eggs and sugar until smooth.
  4. Add the butter, lemon juice and lemon zest.
  5. Add the flour, baking powder and salt.
  6. Mix everything until you have a smooth batter.
  7. Pour the batter into a cake pan.
  8. Bake for 30–35 minutes.
  9. Let the cake cool before serving.

My tip

For an extra lemony flavor, add some fresh lemon zest on top of the cake before serving.
